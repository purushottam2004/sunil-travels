# The site shows the rate

The page shows the day rate, the transfer rate, the day-package price, or the kilometre rate. It does not multiply that rate by days, packages, or kilometres. A multiplied figure would read as a quote, and an outstation figure would need a distance the site does not have. The dates, the number of day packages, and the destination travel in the WhatsApp message, and the agreed price is made on the call.
