# WhatsApp is the desk

Every enquiry is stored and also opened in WhatsApp with the message already written. There is no staff inbox, because the hire is confirmed offline. A signed-in visitor can read their own enquiries, as a copy of what they sent, with no confirmed or declined status. A guest's enquiry is stored and is not listed under an account. Every enquiry includes the visitor's callback phone. The business phone number stays on the page for someone who will only call.
