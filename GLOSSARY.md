# Sunil Travels

Sunil Travels is the working public name of a local Pune business. The hire happens offline. The site publishes the catalog and lets a visitor send an enquiry.

## Language

### Offers

**Vehicle**:
A named model in the catalog, such as a Creta. Every physical car of that model is the same vehicle. It may be offered as a self-drive rental, a chauffeur trip, or both.
_Avoid_: Plate, unit

**Self-drive rental**:
A vehicle the visitor drives, for a pickup time and a return time, at a day rate.
_Avoid_: Cab, tour, package, subscription

**Chauffeur trip**:
A vehicle with a driver. It is an airport transfer, a local trip, or an outstation trip.
_Avoid_: Self-drive, tour, package

**Airport transfer**:
A chauffeur trip between Pune airport and a meetup, at a transfer rate, in either direction. A trip to another city's airport is an outstation trip.
_Avoid_: Local trip, outstation trip

**Local trip**:
A chauffeur trip that stays inside Pune, from one meetup, priced as one or more day packages.
_Avoid_: Airport transfer, outstation trip

**Outstation trip**:
A chauffeur trip from a meetup in Pune to a named place outside Pune, with a start and a return, priced at a kilometre rate for the round trip.
_Avoid_: Airport transfer, local trip

**Meetup**:
The one address where the driver arrives for a chauffeur trip.
_Avoid_: Handover, drop, yard

**Catalog**:
The published list of vehicles Sunil Travels offers. A chosen date does not add or remove a vehicle.
_Avoid_: Inventory, availability

**Enquiry**:
A visitor's request for one vehicle, including a callback phone, kept by the site and sent on WhatsApp. A person confirms it offline, and the visitor can see it only when they sent it while signed in.
_Avoid_: Booking, reservation, order

**Visitor**:
A person who browses the catalog and may send an enquiry.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, account

**Account**:
An optional login a visitor opens to see enquiries they sent while signed in. Sending an enquiry does not require one, and a guest enquiry is never added afterwards.
_Avoid_: Customer, profile

### Money

**Starting price**:
The rate published for a hire. The site shows that rate, and the price agreed afterwards can be different.
_Avoid_: Fare, quote, total, booking amount

**Day rate**:
The self-drive starting price for one day.
_Avoid_: Hourly rate

**Transfer rate**:
The starting price for one airport transfer.
_Avoid_: Day rate, kilometre rate

**Day package**:
The starting price of a local trip for 8 hours and 80 km inside Pune. Time or distance past that is agreed offline.
_Avoid_: Day rate, transfer rate

**Kilometre rate**:
The outstation starting price for one kilometre of the round trip.
_Avoid_: Day rate, transfer rate

### Handover

**Handover**:
The visitor's choice on a self-drive rental: collection or delivery.
_Avoid_: Pickup

**Collection**:
The visitor takes the vehicle from the yard.
_Avoid_: Delivery

**Delivery**:
Sunil Travels brings the vehicle to an address the visitor names.
_Avoid_: Collection

**Yard**:
The one Pune place where a visitor collects a self-drive vehicle.
_Avoid_: Branch, hub
